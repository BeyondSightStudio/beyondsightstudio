​​Welcome to Beyond Si​ght Studio 


Beyond Sight Studio is an independent game and asset studio focused on art, games, music, and production-ready resources for creators. I develop two types of asset packs:

🌟 ​Premium ​Assets Packs - It has more assets in the pack and more resources for bigger projects.
🌱 Lite Assets Packs - Good enough for lone developers, small teams, and small projects for beginners.

​​Every release is built to support worldbuilding, reduce production friction, and help turn ideas into finished work. There are no subscriptions or hidden models just straightforward, ready-to-use assets. In addition to asset creation, I develop original indie games inspired by the same fantasy-driven worlds behind my resources. To explore available assets and games.

​❓Q1: Can I use your free assets in commercial projects?

Yes! Our free “lite” assets are released under a commercial-friendly license. You’re free to use them in both free and paid projects — credit to Beyond Sight Studio isn’t required, but it’s always appreciated as a sign of appreciation for our work.

​❓ Q2: Can I edit or modify your assets to fit my game?

Absolutely. You’re welcome to modify, recolor, or adapt our assets to match your game’s style — whether it’s a free lite pack or a premium one. Just remember: redistribution of modified assets is not allowed.

​❓ Q3: Do you take custom asset or music requests? How to get future updates?

At this time, we’re focused on our own creative projects and releasing polished asset packs — so we don’t offer commissions or custom work. However, we’re open to suggestions for future packs. Feel free to email us your ideas!
